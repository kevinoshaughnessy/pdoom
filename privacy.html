Privacy notice

Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: your answers are only sent anywhere if you choose Save my answers at the end of the quiz. Saved answers are used to study how people reason about AI risk and are deleted after three years. Your name is optional. You can ask for your answers to be deleted at any time.

Who is responsible

This calculator is run by Kevin O’Shaughnessy, an individual based in the UK, who is the controller of your data under the UK GDPR. Contact: kevin.mark.oshaughnessy@gmail.com.

What is collected

Only if you choose Save my answers, the following is stored:

  • your answers to the questions, your own p(Doom) estimates before and after, and the calculated result;
  • details of your quiz run: the order and wording the questions were shown in, when you started and finished, the quiz version, and a random response ID;
  • if you fill them in: your name, your background, and your explanation of why your number differs from the calculation.

If you choose Keep my answers private, none of this is sent. The quiz itself doesn’t record your IP address, and it uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Please don’t write anything in the free-text box that identifies other people.

On your device: your progress is kept in your browser’s local storage so you can pick up where you left off. It stays on your device. “Start again” clears your answers, and clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it completely.

Why, and the lawful basis

Saved answers are used to study how people reason about AI risk: how the individual answers relate to people’s overall estimates, and whether the order or wording of questions changes them. Results may be published, but only in aggregate or anonymised form. Names are never published.

The lawful basis is your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by choosing Save my answers. Your answers and free text may reveal political or philosophical opinions; to the extent they do, they are processed on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). Saving is entirely voluntary: you can use the whole quiz and see your result without saving anything.

Who else handles it

  • Google stores saved answers, in a Google Sheet in my Google account, received through Google Apps Script.
  • GitHub (part of Microsoft) hosts this website. Like any web host, its servers receive your IP address when you load the page and may log it for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your answers are not sold or shared with anyone else, and are not used for marketing.

Transfers outside the UK

Google and GitHub may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the “UK–US data bridge”), which the UK Government recognises as providing adequate protection. For the safeguards each company uses, see Google’s legal frameworks for data transfers and GitHub’s privacy statement.

How long it is kept

Saved answers are kept for three years from the date you submit them, then deleted. Aggregate or fully anonymised results, which can’t identify you, may be kept after that.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • withdraw your consent at any time, which means your saved answers are deleted (this doesn’t affect anything done with them before you withdrew);
  • see the data held about you, and get a copy in a portable format;
  • have it corrected or deleted, or its use restricted.

To do any of these, email kevin.mark.oshaughnessy@gmail.com with your response ID, which is shown after you save your answers. If you didn’t note it, give the approximate date and time you took the quiz and, if you entered one, your name. You’ll get a reply within one month.

You can also change your saved answers yourself: use “Change my answers” in the same browser and save again, and your earlier answers are replaced.

No decisions are made about you from your answers. The calculator works out a number for you to see, and that’s all.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how your data is handled, please get in touch first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will be updated. If the way saved answers are used changes significantly, the change will be announced on the quiz page.
